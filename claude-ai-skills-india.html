What Indian professionals actually need from Claude

The generic AI tools that work in the US often fall short in the Indian professional context. The problems are specific:

  • LinkedIn posts — Indian professionals want posts that build credibility with international audiences, not just local reach. Generic AI output tends toward generic inspiration content that blends in.
  • Cold email — Outreach to US and European clients or investors requires precise framing. A message that sounds fine to a local audience can read as indirect or vague to a Western recipient. Getting the right tone calibration matters more than getting the words out fast.
  • Pitch decks — Indian startup founders pitching to global VCs face a specific challenge: the traction signals that matter in India (scale, user numbers, unit economics) need to be framed in terms that resonate with a San Francisco or London LP base.
  • CVs and cover letters — Candidates applying to MNCs or global remote roles need output that matches international formatting expectations, not the Indian resume style most local tools default to.

Writing your own Claude prompts from scratch can produce decent results — eventually. The problem is it takes 30 minutes of iteration per document to get there.
