What are Claude AI marketing skills?

NovaKit marketing skills are .skill files you upload into Claude once, each one built for a single marketing deliverable — a paid social ad set, an Amazon listing, a carousel, a press release, a product photography prompt. Instead of a blank chat, you get a framework that runs live research on what is performing on that specific platform, asks three calibrating questions about your product, buyer and funnel stage, then applies an anti-slop gate that blocks the phrasing people scroll past.

This collection is for the person who owns the output: solo marketers, DTC and ecommerce founders, agency copywriters, creators, and small in-house teams who ship across five channels a week with no time for five separate briefs. The outcome is the full asset, not a fragment — the Brand & Ad Copy skill returns hooks, Meta variants, a Google RSA block, LinkedIn and TikTok copy, nine CTAs and an A/B plan in one run. Skills start at $5 and work on a free Claude account. The Marketing Bundle collects five core skills for $45 instead of $65.

Quick Answers

How are these delivered, and how long does setup take?

Checkout takes you to a delivery page with a download link for a .skill file, emailed to you as well. In Claude, go to Customize → Skills → +, choose Upload a skill, and drop the file in. It appears in your skills list right away and runs as a slash command — type / in any chat and select it. Setup is under 60 seconds, and the skill works on claude.ai and the Claude desktop app.

Do I need Claude Pro or an API key?

No. Every marketing skill here runs on a free Claude.ai account with no API key and no other subscription. Pro helps only when you want the longest single outputs — a full 30/60/90-day content calendar or a complete brand voice guide — because it allows longer responses. Nothing on this page requires it.

How is this different from saving a good prompt?

A saved prompt is frozen at the moment you wrote it. These skills run live research before writing, so what they treat as "working right now" updates with the market, then they interview you before producing anything. They also enforce platform mechanics a prompt would not remember: Google RSA headlines under 30 characters, email subject lines under 45, Fair Housing compliance in real estate listing copy, Amazon backend search terms in product listings.

Which marketing skill should I start with?

Pick by channel. Running paid social: Brand & Ad Copy for the copy and UGC Ad Creator for the creative plan. Selling products online: E-Commerce Product Listing plus Product Photography Prompt. Building organic presence: Social Content Engine, LinkedIn Post Engine, or Content Calendar. Setting the foundation before writing anything: Brand Voice Guide.

Do the AI image and video prompt skills need a paid generation tool?

The skill itself runs in Claude and outputs the prompt, so Claude costs nothing. Generating the image or video happens in whichever tool you already use — Midjourney, Flux, DALL·E, Ideogram, Firefly, Kling, Seedance or Higgsfield — and those charge their own credits. That is the point of buying the prompt skill: AI Video Prompt ships a negative prompt and a tool disclaimer specifically so you burn fewer credits on junk frames.
