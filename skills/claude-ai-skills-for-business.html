Why business documents need audience calibration

A pitch deck for a pre-seed investor is not the document a Series A investor reads. A business plan for a bank assessing lending risk is not the plan an accelerator assesses for team and traction. An NDA for a freelance contractor is not the NDA for a technology partnership. Generic Claude does not know the difference and produces the same document either way. NovaKit business skills are .skill files that ask three calibrating questions — who reads this, at what stage, with what at stake — before a word is written, then run an anti-slop gate over the result.

This collection is for founders, product managers, operators and consultants: the people who have to produce an investor-grade document on a deadline with no in-house finance, legal or product-marketing function. The outcome is a document built for the person on the other side of the table, complete enough to send — with attorney-review flags where a lawyer genuinely needs to look, as in the NDA / Contract Draft and Terms & Privacy Policy skills. Skills start at $5. The Founder Bundle gets six core business skills for $39 instead of $75.

The skills fall into four jobs. Raising money: pitch deck narrative, business plan, investor update email, and the financial model prompt that turns your assumptions into a paste-ready Excel or Sheets build. Selling: cold outreach email and sales landing page copy, both calibrated to buyer awareness rather than product features. Building: the PRD writer, the architecture diagram prompt, and the job description writer with its inclusive-language audit. Protecting: NDA and contract drafting, terms of service and privacy policy, and performance reviews written to be evidence-based and legally defensible. Four more — book cover, home renovation brief, recipe development, and travel itinerary — cover the professional documents solo operators in those trades actually bill for.

Quick Answers

What's the difference between using Claude directly and using a NovaKit business skill?

Generic Claude writes the same document regardless of who reads it. A NovaKit skill asks three calibrating questions first — audience, stage, and stakes — and changes the structure accordingly. Pitch Deck Narrative writes a different deck for pre-seed than for Series B, and a different one for a VC than for a corporate strategic. Business Plan calculates DSCR for a bank plan and drops it for an investor plan. Each skill also runs an anti-slop gate that rewrites disqualifying phrasing before you see the draft.

How is the skill delivered and installed?

After checkout you get a delivery page with a download link for a .skill file, plus the same link by email. In Claude, open Customize → Skills → +, choose Upload a skill, and drag the file in. It shows up in your skills list immediately and runs as a slash command — type / in a new chat and select it. Install takes under 60 seconds and works on claude.ai and the desktop app.

Do these skills work with the free Claude plan?

Yes. All NovaKit business skills run on a free Claude.ai account — no Claude Pro, no API key, no subscription beyond Claude's free tier. Pro is worth having for the longest documents, since a 12-section business plan or a full Terms of Service and Privacy Policy pair produces a lot of output in one response, but it is not required.

Can I rely on the legal and financial documents?

Treat them as strong first drafts, not as advice. The NDA / Contract Draft and ToS & Privacy Policy skills label every clause, add a plain-English summary under each one, flag every non-standard or high-risk provision for attorney review, and mark jurisdiction issues for GDPR, CCPA, multi-state US and UK. Business Plan and Performance Review Writer ship with mandatory disclaimers. The point is to make your lawyer's review shorter, not to skip it.

Which skill should I start with as a first-time founder?

Start with Pitch Deck Narrative if you are raising — it is the document most founders rewrite five times and still get wrong. If you are earlier and validating the model, Business Plan structures the thinking before you commit resources. Building product rather than raising? Start with PRD Writer.
