Why not just use ChatGPT for property listings?

You can, and plenty of agents already do. The difference is where the work happens. With ChatGPT you leave WhatsApp, open another app, type the same details again, and paste the reply back. NovaKit sits inside the WhatsApp number you already use all day, so there's no app-switching between a site visit and the next call. It also won't invent a detail it wasn't given — something a general AI does regularly when a fact is missing.

Does NovaKit ever make up details in a listing?

No. If you don't mention the carpet area, the floor, or the price, it asks you for that instead of guessing a number that sounds plausible. Every listing it writes only uses facts you actually sent it — nothing is filled in from a general sense of what a similar property might have.

Is NovaKit compliant with RERA?

Every listing is checked against RERA rules before it's sent back to you — things like not promising guaranteed returns or appreciation, and including what's legally required in the ad. It's a check that runs automatically on every message, not something you have to remember to ask for.

Does it understand Hindi, or only English?

Both, and the mix most people actually speak. Send a voice note in Hindi, English, or Hinglish — the way you'd naturally describe a property to a colleague — and you'll get a clean, ready listing back in text.
