NovaKit for property management companies

What genuinely fits for a PG, serviced-apartment, or long-term rental portfolio — and where NovaKit isn't the right tool at all. Both matter more than a features list.

Photo: Rakesh Rabha / Pexels

Does it support PG and serviced-apartment listings, or only sale listings?

Yes — PG, serviced apartment, and farmhouse are supported as property types within the same listing writer that handles sale listings, not a separate product you'd have to buy into. The compliance check is scoped to match: a PG or serviced-apartment listing correctly doesn't get a RERA disclaimer forced onto it, since that requirement is for registered-project sales, not this kind of accommodation.

Does it handle short-term, Airbnb-style nightly rentals?

No — and this is worth being direct about rather than glossing over. A dedicated module for nightly-rental hosting was actually built at one point, then deliberately taken down. It shared the same compliance gate as the real estate listing writer, which was applying RERA carpet-area rules that don't make sense for a nightly stay — rather than ship a compliance check that didn't fit, the module was pulled. If your portfolio is long-term rentals, PGs, or serviced apartments, the live listing writer fits. If it's short-term nightly stays, NovaKit isn't the right tool today.

Fits today

  • PG and co-living listings
  • Serviced apartments
  • Farmhouses, long-term rentals
  • Enquiry replies for any of the above

Doesn't fit

  • Airbnb-style nightly stays
  • Rent collection or ledgers
  • Maintenance ticketing
  • Tenant records / a CRM

Can a team of property managers share one account?

Yes, on Studio — up to 5 people share one account, with usage tracked per person, which fits a small portfolio-management team better than a single agent's Solo plan. Everyone still works inside their own WhatsApp conversation with the same shared kit, rather than one number everyone crowds into.

What this doesn't do

  • No rent collection, ledgers, or payment tracking of any kind.
  • No maintenance ticketing or tenant issue tracking.
  • No tenant database or lease-document management — this is a writing tool, not a property management system.

If you're managing a portfolio of PGs, serviced apartments, or long-term rentals and the actual bottleneck is the writing — listings, enquiry replies — this fits. If the bottleneck is tracking payments, maintenance, or leases, that's a different category of tool entirely, and this page won't pretend otherwise.
