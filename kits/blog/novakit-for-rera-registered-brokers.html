NovaKit for RERA-registered brokers

What actually changes when you're a registered agent using NovaKit — your registration number, what gets checked automatically, and what stays entirely on you.

Photo: Ketut Subiyanto / Pexels

This isn't legal advice. It's a plain description of what NovaKit does and doesn't do relative to your own RERA obligations. See RERA rules for advertising a property on WhatsApp for the underlying law and sources.

Does using NovaKit change any of my RERA duties as an agent?

No. Section 9's requirement that a registered agent facilitate only registered-project transactions, and Section 10's bar on false statements and unfair trade practices, are your own obligations — they exist independent of whatever tool you use to write a message. NovaKit doesn't register you, doesn't check your registration status, and doesn't verify that a project you're listing is actually registered. It only affects the wording of what you send, not your standing as a broker.

How does my RERA number actually get into a listing?

You tell it once. Send your RERA number as a plain message, and it's saved to your profile from that point on — every listing generated afterward includes it automatically, the way Section 11(1)(a) requires. You're not retyping it into every message, and if it changes, you update it the same way.

my rera number is HR/GGM/1234/2026, add it to all my listings going forward
Saved. Every listing from now on will include this automatically — no need to add it yourself.

What does it actually check automatically, versus what's on me?

It checks the wording it generates against known misleading patterns — no guaranteed-return or assured-appreciation language — and includes your RERA number where your profile has one on file. That's the automatic part. What it doesn't do: verify the project you're listing is registered, verify your own agent registration under Section 9, or give legal advice on whether a specific listing is compliant in your state. The wording check runs on every message; knowing your own registration status is entirely yours.

buyer wants guaranteed 12% returns, can you promise that in the listing
I can't include a guaranteed-return line — that's the kind of claim Section 12 treats as misleading, and it puts you on the hook if it doesn't hold up. Want me to write the actual rental yield history instead, if you have it?✓ Blocked before it was sent

Is this a compliance tool, or a writing tool?

A writing tool with one compliance-shaped guardrail built in — not a compliance tool. It won't stop you from listing an unregistered project, because it has no way to know a project's registration status; that check has to happen on your end, the same as it always has. What it does reduce is one specific, controllable risk: the wording itself carrying a claim that gets a buyer their money back with interest. That's the part a writing tool can actually reach.

In short, for a RERA-registered broker

  • Changes: your RERA number is set once and included automatically; guaranteed-return language is blocked before it's sent.
  • Doesn't change: your own Section 9 registration, your duty to only facilitate registered-project sales, your duty under Section 10 not to misrepresent an affiliation you don't have.
