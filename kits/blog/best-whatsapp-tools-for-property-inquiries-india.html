Best WhatsApp tools for property inquiries in India

An honest look at NovaKit alongside Leadrat, Kraya AI, and Zoho CRM — three real, named tools agents actually use. Real tradeoffs for all four, this one included.

Why these three. They represent three different approaches agents actually use for WhatsApp and lead handling in India: a real-estate-specific CRM (Leadrat), a WhatsApp-native automation CRM (Kraya AI), and a general-purpose CRM configured for real estate (Zoho CRM). Pricing and features below are pulled from each company's own site as of writing — check their current pages before deciding, since SaaS pricing changes.

These aren't all the same kind of tool

That's the first thing worth being honest about. Leadrat, Kraya AI, and Zoho CRM are lead-pipeline systems — they track where a lead came from, what stage a deal is in, who's supposed to follow up next. NovaKit doesn't do any of that. It's a WhatsApp writing tool: it drafts the listing and the objection reply, checked against RERA-style rules, and stops there. If you're comparing these on a feature grid, you're really asking two separate questions — "what tracks my leads" and "what writes my messages" — worth answering separately rather than picking one winner.

Real-estate CRM

Leadrat

Centralizes leads from portals and social media, tracks pipeline stages, automates appointment scheduling, and gives teams performance analytics. Built specifically for Indian and Gulf-market brokers and developers.

StrengthAggregates leads across sources — portals, social, website — into one pipeline view.
LimitWhatsApp is one integrated channel, not the native interface. No published pricing — demo required for a quote.
WhatsApp-native CRM

Kraya AI

Pulls leads from 99acres, MagicBricks, and Facebook Lead Ads directly into WhatsApp, automates a sub-60-second first response, and tracks pipeline stages with team routing — all inside WhatsApp itself.

StrengthWhatsApp-first design, fast automated first response, flat ₹2,999/month for unlimited agents.
LimitMeta's own per-conversation API fees (₹0.30–0.75) are billed on top — and it automates follow-up, not the actual copy of a listing or objection reply.
General-purpose CRM

Zoho CRM

Not built for real estate specifically, but widely configured for it by Indian agencies. Integrates with the WhatsApp Business API for automated follow-ups and reminders.

StrengthFlexible, well-supported, transparent published pricing (₹800–2,600/user/month, billed annually + GST).
LimitNo real-estate- or RERA-specific behavior out of the box — you configure it yourself, or pay an implementation partner to.
WhatsApp writing tool

NovaKit

Writes the listing and the objection reply from what you type or say, checked against RERA-style rules before anything is sent. Lives inside the same WhatsApp you already use — no separate app, no login.

StrengthOnly tool here that writes the actual message content, not just automates who gets it and when. Works in Hindi and English.
LimitNot a CRM — no lead pipeline, no portal aggregation, no automated follow-up sequencing. Two modules live today, not a full sales stack.

The honest answer

For a lot of teams, the real answer isn't "which one" — it's a CRM for the pipeline plus NovaKit for what actually gets typed into WhatsApp, since none of the three CRMs above draft the message itself. If you're a solo agent who mostly needs the writing handled and doesn't have a multi-source lead pipeline to manage yet, NovaKit alone may be enough. If you're running a team drowning in leads from five portals, a pipeline tool like Leadrat or Kraya AI solves a problem NovaKit was never built to solve.
