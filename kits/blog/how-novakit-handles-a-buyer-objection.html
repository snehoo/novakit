How NovaKit replies to a buyer objection

The second live module in the real estate kit. What you send it, what comes back on Solo versus Studio, and why it refuses to invent a comparable sale just to win an argument.

Photo: Ketut Subiyanto / Pexels

What do I actually send it?

Just what the buyer said, in your own words — "buyer says 85 lac is too high" is enough. You don't categorize the objection or fill in a form. Because this runs in the same conversation as the listing you already generated, it already knows the property's real facts and uses those, not a template.

buyer says 85 lac is too high for a 1200 sqft 2bhk

What comes back?

A script you can send as-is, built from the property's own listed facts — not a market comparable it wasn't given, and not a stat it made up to sound more convincing.

Primary script
Totally fair to ask. At ₹85 Lac for 1,200 sq ft carpet, you're getting a modular kitchen with hob and chimney already fitted, built-in mirrored wardrobes, and a balcony — the kind of finishing that usually adds a separate line item after possession. Want to walk through it in person before deciding on the number?

What's different between Solo and Studio?

Solo gives you one ready reply plus a fallback if the first doesn't land — enough for most single-agent conversations. Studio gives you the full set the underlying script is built to produce: the primary reply, two fallback angles, a version to send before the objection comes up, a short note on when to soften versus hold firm, and a suggested next move. Same underlying facts, more of the shape around them.

Solo

  • Primary script
  • One fallback variant
  • No memory across messages

Studio

  • Primary script
  • Two fallback variants
  • Pre-emption version
  • Tone guide
  • Follow-up move
  • Remembers last 5 exchanges

Does it remember the earlier conversation?

On Studio, yes. If the same buyer messages again a day later, it responds with that history in mind instead of making you re-explain the property. On Solo, each objection is handled on its own — still useful for a one-off reply, just without the thread of memory.

still thinking about it, will let you know
No pressure — if the modular kitchen and the balcony were the parts that stood out earlier, happy to send a couple more angles from Sector 92 whenever you're ready.
Studio only — replies with the earlier conversation in mind

What this doesn't do

  • Doesn't invent a comparable sale, market stat, or price trend it wasn't given.
  • Doesn't send the reply for you — every script comes back to you on WhatsApp to review and send.
  • Doesn't negotiate or commit to a price on your behalf — it drafts the words, you make the call.

Like the listing writer, this is a module that's actually built and live — not a roadmap item. The kits page has the full list of what's live today versus what's still being explored.
