Does NovaKit work the same in every Indian city?

Same product, same price, everywhere on WhatsApp. Two things genuinely vary by where you are — which RERA authority applies to you, and how much the Hindi/English-only language support actually covers your conversations.

Photo: Shantum Singh / Pexels

Does it work differently in Mumbai, Bangalore, Delhi, or Gurgaon?

No — the product, the price, and the modules are identical wherever you are in India. WhatsApp doesn't know or care what city you're in, and neither does the listing writer or the objection handler. There's no city-tier pricing, and nothing about the plan changes based on your pin code. What actually varies is outside the product: which RERA authority you're registered with, and how much of your day-to-day conversation actually happens in Hindi.

Does the RERA check know which state I'm in?

No. RERA is implemented by each state through its own authority, not one national body — a few examples below. NovaKit's compliance check is one generic, category-scoped gate, not a state-by-state rules engine. It blocks the same misleading-claim patterns (guaranteed returns, assured appreciation) regardless of which state you're in, and includes your RERA number wherever your profile has one on file. It doesn't apply state-specific procedural differences beyond that — for those, your own state authority's site is the source of truth.

Does it work in languages other than Hindi and English?

No, and this matters more in some cities than others. Voice and text support covers Hindi and English — not Kannada, Tamil, Bengali, Telugu, or Marathi. For an agent in Gurgaon or Mumbai working mostly in Hindi and English, that's rarely a gap in practice. For an agent in Chennai or Bengaluru whose buyers default to Tamil or Kannada, it's a real limitation worth knowing about before relying on it for every conversation — not something to discover after the trial.

Does my RERA number change depending on my state's authority?

No — you give it your RERA number once, whichever state authority issued it, and it's included in every listing from then on, the same way no matter which authority you're registered with. NovaKit doesn't validate the number against your state's portal or check that it's current; it includes what you've told it, nothing more. Verifying the number itself is accurate is still on you — the same as it always was before any tool was involved.

In short

  • Same everywhere: product, plans, pricing, modules, the RERA-style wording check.
  • Varies by you, not the product: which state RERA authority you're actually registered with.
  • A real limitation, more relevant in some cities: Hindi and English only — no Kannada, Tamil, Bengali, Telugu, or Marathi.
